Simulation Directives

OBJECTIVE: Traverse the overworld and conquer reality nodes.

MOVEMENT: Use W A S D or Arrow Keys to move your avatar.

INTERACTION: Walk over a Node and press 'E' to enter.

ENEMIES: Hostile red avatars roam the map — they will intercept you!

ESCAPE: Press ESC to exit a node or close panels.

COMMS: Hold V for push-to-talk voice or use the chat panel.

💡 HINTS: Hover over any action button to see a description of what it does before committing to it.
